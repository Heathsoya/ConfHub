# ConfHub

**Academic Conference Management System**

ConfHub is an integrated academic conference management system designed to streamline the organization and management of academic conferences. It provides a centralized platform for conference information publishing, paper submission, peer review, academic communication, and participant registration.

## Overview

ConfHub aims to simplify the academic conference workflow by connecting conference organizers, reviewers, and paper authors through a unified platform.

The system supports three primary user roles:

- **Administrator** — Manages conference information, users, papers, reviewers, notifications, and conference activities.
- **Reviewer / Expert** — Reviews submitted papers, manages personal information, and provides review feedback.
- **Author** — Registers accounts, submits papers, manages author information, and tracks submission status.

## Features

### 1. Conference Information

- Conference introduction and announcements
- Conference topics and submission guidelines
- Important dates and deadlines
- Organizing and sponsoring institution information
- Conference materials and document downloads
- Expert and committee member introductions

### 2. Paper Submission

- Author registration and account management
- Online paper submission
- Support for multiple submissions under one account
- Paper information management
- Chinese and English titles and abstracts
- Multilingual research keywords
- Multiple authors and affiliations
- First-author and corresponding-author designation
- Author ordering and information management
- Paper preview, download, modification, and deletion
- Supplementary file uploads

### 3. Peer Review

- Reviewer account management
- Reviewer research interests and keyword configuration
- Automatic reviewer assignment based on paper topics
- Administrator-adjustable reviewer-paper assignments
- Online paper evaluation
- Structured review forms
- Review comments and publication recommendations

### 4. Conference Management

- Conference date management
- Conference materials management
- Keynote speaker management
- Special topic and session management
- Conference schedule management
- Announcement and notification management

### 5. Administration

- User and role management
- Paper and submission status management
- Reviewer management and batch import
- Review assignment management
- Dynamic conference content management
- Notification and document management

## User Roles

| Role | Main Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Administrator | System configuration, conference management, user management, paper management, reviewer assignment |
| Reviewer | Paper evaluation, review comments, personal information management |
| Author | Account registration, paper submission, author management, submission tracking |
